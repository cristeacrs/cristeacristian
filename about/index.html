About Me

Hi, I'm Cristian Cristea

I'm a Software Developer with experience in building, maintaining, and modernizing enterprise applications. I focus on frontend development, intuitive user interfaces, and reliable business solutions.

I currently work with JavaScript and the Servoy platform , developing data-driven applications, implementing business workflows, and modernizing legacy interfaces. My technical background also includes TypeScript, React, Node.js, and SQL databases .

I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at the West University of Timișoara, where I also pursued a Master's degree in Software Engineering. Before transitioning into software development, I worked as a Network Performance Engineer at Nokia, gaining valuable experience in troubleshooting, technical analysis, and complex systems.

I'm passionate about modern web technologies and continuous learning. My goal is to create efficient, maintainable, and user-friendly applications while growing further in React, TypeScript, and full-stack development .
